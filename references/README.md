# Annotated references

1. **BTS TranStats: Reporting Carrier On-Time Performance.** Official dataset description and source. Lists flight-level operations, scheduled and actual times, delays, cancellations/diversions, and distance. https://www.transtats.bts.gov/TableInfo.asp?QO_fu146_anzr=b0-gvzr&gnoyr_VQ=FGJ
2. **BTS Technical Directive No. 39: Reporting On-Time Performance 2025.** Official 2025 reporting scope and data requirements, including tail numbers and local time fields. https://www.bts.gov/explore-topics-and-geography/modes/aviation/number-39-technical-directive-reporting-time
3. **DOT Office of Inspector General, AV2025003 (2024).** Found BTS had automated checks for accuracy but no method to verify all flights were reported, a material caveat for chain reconstruction. https://www.oig.dot.gov/library-item/46490?page=1
4. **Xu et al., “Aeolus: A Multi-structural Flight Delay Dataset,” NeurIPS 2025 Datasets and Benchmarks.** Existing 50M+ flight benchmark with tabular, chain, and network structures, temporal splits and leakage prevention. It means AeroPulse cannot claim novelty for flight-chain prediction itself. https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/586fbdff064d506f5af3e3db82681f84-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html
5. **Aeolus code repository.** Reproducible preprocessing and benchmark experiments; dataset is hosted separately. Review license/terms before reusing. https://github.com/Flnny/Delay-data
6. **“Flight delay propagation modeling: Data, Methods, and Future Opportunities,” Transportation Research Part E (2024), DOI 10.1016/j.tre.2024.103525.** Review of existing chain/network approaches; supports positioning this as careful replication and evaluation, not an entirely unexplored idea. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1366554524001169
